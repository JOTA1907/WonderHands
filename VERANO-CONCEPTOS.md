## Prompts utilizados

### Brisa

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: WonderHands summer concept catalog photo, design Brisa. Create an original photorealistic editorial fashion photograph in portrait 4:5. One adult model wears a relaxed ivory cotton hand-crocheted summer blouse with short sleeves, round neckline, hip-length straight hem, and a large airy diamond openwork stitch pattern. The whole blouse, sleeves, and hem are visible; modest ivory camisole beneath. The crochet loops and individual yarn strands are distinct, with subtle human stitch variation, unmistakably crafted by hand rather than fine uniform machine knit. Warm off-white studio wall, soft natural daylight, neutral linen trousers, minimal styling, elegant but believable garment construction. No text, logos, labels, watermark, extra accessories, synthetic plastic texture, fused or impossible stitches. This is a concept image, not a photo of a real product.

### Marea

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: WonderHands summer concept catalog photo, design Marea. Create an original photorealistic editorial fashion photograph in portrait 4:5. One adult model wears a seafoam green hand-crocheted sleeveless halter top, high rounded halter neckline, exposed shoulders, relaxed waist-length fit, scalloped lower edge, and a tactile fan-stitch pattern with visible medium-size crochet loops. The entire top from neckline to hem is visible, styled with neutral high-waisted linen trousers. Cotton yarn and small natural differences in hand-worked stitch tension should read as genuinely handcrafted, never fine flat machine knit. Warm off-white studio wall, soft window daylight, minimal editorial styling, accurate wearable construction and symmetrical neckline. No text, logos, labels, watermark, beach props, synthetic plastic texture, tiny uniform stitches, or impossible seams. This is a concept image, not a photo of a real product.

### Dunas

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: WonderHands summer concept catalog photo, design Dunas. Create an original photorealistic editorial fashion photograph in portrait 4:5. Full-length view of one adult model wearing a sand-beige hand-crocheted sleeveless summer midi dress with wide shoulder straps, softly rounded neckline, gently flared skirt ending below the knee, and a clearly visible openwork shell-and-diamond crochet pattern. A simple matching opaque slip sits underneath for modesty. Show the whole dress from shoulders to hem with room around it, model standing naturally. Medium cotton yarn, distinct hand-crocheted loops and subtle human stitch variation, unmistakably handmade rather than fine uniform machine knit. Warm off-white studio wall, soft natural daylight, quiet editorial styling. Coherent wearable construction, symmetrical straps and hem, realistic gravity. No text, logos, labels, watermark, beach props, synthetic plastic texture, impossible stitches, or cropped garment. This is a concept image, not a photo of a real product.
